# Privacy Policy

Effective October 10, 2026

YouTube Age Filter processes the YouTube page displayed in your browser to decide which video cards to hide. It does not collect or transmit browsing history, account information, video activity, or extension settings. It contains no analytics, telemetry, advertising, remote code, or external service integration.

## Local settings

The maximum video age, channel exceptions, Shorts preference, pause state, and diagnostic preference are saved through the browser's local extension storage. They are not synchronized by this extension or sent to the project maintainers. Uninstalling the extension removes its local extension storage according to the browser's uninstall behavior.

## Permissions

| Permission | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `storage` | Save preferences on your device. |
| `https://www.youtube.com/*` | Read video metadata and hide matching elements on desktop YouTube pages. |

The popup queries the active tab identifier to request its current channel from the extension's content script. No tab history is collected or stored.

## Third parties

YouTube continues to operate under its own terms and privacy policy. Installing this extension does not change YouTube's data practices. GitHub issues and discussions are public; do not include private account details, browsing history, cookies, or access tokens in reports.

## Contact

For privacy questions, use the repository's [issue tracker](https://github.com/digitalrcs/youtube-age-filter/issues). For security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
